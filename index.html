Agency FAQ

Who can apply?

Marketing agencies, freelancers, media buyers and growth consultants who manage or plan to manage multiple e-commerce clients in the UAE / GCC.

How is commission calculated?

You earn a percentage of the profit from every delivered order placed by your clients. Exact rates depend on your plan (Gold / Diamond) and volume.

When do I get paid?

Same as regular sellers — every Friday. Commission from client orders is included in your weekly payout.

Do clients need their own accounts?

No. You manage everything under your agency account. Clients can optionally get view-only access if needed.
